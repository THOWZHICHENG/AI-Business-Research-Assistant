[Document 1: Customer Problems]

Small businesses may struggle to establish an online presence because
they lack technical expertise, website maintenance resources, and
knowledge of user experience design.

Some business owners may find it difficult to communicate their
requirements to website developers.


[Document 2: Market Opportunities]

Potential opportunities for website design agencies include helping
small businesses establish an online presence, improving mobile user
experience, and providing website maintenance services.

Businesses may also benefit from search engine optimization and
e-commerce integration.


[Document 3: Startup Challenges]

Starting a website design agency requires technical skills, portfolio
development, client acquisition, and project management.

New agencies may face competition from established companies,
freelancers, and automated website builders.


[Document 4: Customer Expectations]

Customers may expect responsive website design, mobile compatibility,
fast loading speed, clear communication, and reliable maintenance.

Some customers may also prioritize affordable pricing and ongoing
technical support.


[Document 5: Business Differentiation]

Website design agencies can differentiate themselves by specializing
in specific industries, offering personalized design solutions,
providing excellent customer support, or developing strong expertise
in user experience design.
